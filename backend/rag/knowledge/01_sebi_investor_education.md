# SEBI investor education: recognising investment fraud

This is a plain-language summary prepared for Suraksha AI from publicly available investor-education guidance by the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI). Always refer to sebi.gov.in and investor.sebi.gov.in for the authoritative version.

## Investments carry risk - nobody can guarantee returns
Investments in the securities market are subject to market risks. No SEBI-registered entity is permitted to promise or guarantee returns. Any person, group, app or advertisement that offers "assured", "fixed", "guaranteed" or "risk-free" returns from the stock market is a strong red flag.

## Unregistered advisers and "tips" groups
Only SEBI-registered Investment Advisers (RIAs) may charge fees for investment advice, and only SEBI-registered Research Analysts (RAs) may publish research reports with recommendations. Before trusting anyone who gives tips:
- Ask for their SEBI registration number.
- Verify it on the SEBI website under "Recognised Intermediaries".
- Check that the name, contact details and validity match.
Registered entities must not promise returns. Paid "VIP" or "premium" WhatsApp and Telegram groups that sell "sure-shot calls" are commonly run by unregistered persons.

## Pump-and-dump schemes
In a pump-and-dump, operators buy a thinly traded stock cheaply, then spread hype through SMS, WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube or social media ("multibagger", "operator buying", "big news coming", "upper circuit"). When ordinary investors buy, the operators sell at inflated prices and the price collapses. SEBI has taken enforcement action against such schemes. Be cautious of unsolicited messages about little-known companies.

## Finfluencers
SEBI has restricted registered intermediaries from associating with unregistered persons who give investment advice or make claims of returns. Social-media "finfluencers" who promise returns or sell tips without registration are not regulated advisers.

## Fake trading apps and websites
Fraudsters create apps or websites that look like trading platforms and show fake profits, then refuse withdrawals or demand "taxes" and "fees" to release money. Trade only through SEBI-registered stockbrokers and verify the app publisher. Use only the official website or official app store listing of a registered broker.

## Payments to registered intermediaries
Make payments only to verified accounts of SEBI-registered intermediaries. SEBI has introduced validated UPI handles (ending in "@valid") for registered intermediaries that collect money from investors, and a verification facility, to help investors confirm they are paying a genuine entity. Never pay into a personal UPI ID or bank account given in a chat.

## Where to complain
- SEBI SCORES (scores.sebi.gov.in) - complaints against SEBI-registered intermediaries and listed companies.
- SMART ODR (smartodr.in) - online dispute resolution for securities market disputes.
- SEBI toll-free investor helpline: 1800 266 7575.
- For cyber fraud and money lost to scammers: call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
