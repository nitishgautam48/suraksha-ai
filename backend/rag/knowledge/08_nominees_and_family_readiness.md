# Nominees and family readiness

This is general awareness material, not legal or financial advice. Rules differ by product and
change over time; confirm details with the bank, depository participant, fund house, insurer or
the official portal concerned.

## Why nominations matter
When an account holder dies, a recorded nominee lets the bank or institution release the money
to a known person without long paperwork. Families often struggle when no nominee is recorded or
when nobody knows which accounts, policies or lockers exist.

## Nominee is not always the final owner
A nominee receives the asset, but in many cases the nominee holds it on behalf of the legal heirs
under a will or succession law. A written will, kept safely and known to the family, reduces
disputes. A lawyer can explain how this applies to a particular family.

## Where to add or check nominees
- **Bank accounts, fixed deposits and lockers**: at the branch or through net banking.
- **Demat and trading accounts**: through the broker or depository participant; these accounts
  need either a nominee or a formal opt-out declaration.
- **Mutual funds**: per folio, through the fund house or its registrar (RTA) website.
- **Insurance**: in the policy itself; tell the family which insurer and where the papers are.
- **EPF**: e-nomination on the EPFO member portal. **PPF**: at the bank or post office.
- **NPS**: through the CRA portal or the bank.
- **Post office savings schemes**: at the post office.

## Keep a simple family record
Write down which banks, policies and schemes exist and where the papers are kept, and tell one
trusted family member. Do not write PINs, passwords or full account numbers on this list.

## Forgotten money
Deposits in bank accounts that have not been used for many years can be searched on the RBI's
UDGAM portal (udgam.rbi.org.in). Unclaimed insurance amounts can be checked with the insurer.

## Beware of "claim agents"
Fraudsters contact families after a death offering to "release" deposits, insurance or shares for
a fee, or ask for OTPs and bank details. Deal only with the institution directly through its
official website or branch.
