# Ponzi, pyramid and fake investment schemes

Summary prepared for Suraksha AI from public investor-awareness guidance.

## How a Ponzi scheme works
A Ponzi scheme pays "returns" to earlier investors using money from new investors rather than from real business profits. It needs a constant inflow of new money and collapses when recruitment slows, leaving most investors with losses.

## Warning signs
- Guaranteed, fixed or unusually high returns (for example "2% daily" or "double your money in 90 days").
- Returns that are consistently high regardless of market conditions.
- Income tied to recruiting others: "referral income", "level income", "binary plan", "team bonus".
- Vague or secret business model ("forex robot", "AI trading bot", "crypto arbitrage") that cannot be verified.
- Pressure to reinvest profits instead of withdrawing, and delays or new "fees" when you try to withdraw.
- No registration with SEBI, RBI or another regulator, or false claims of registration.
- Heavy use of testimonials, luxury lifestyle posts and screenshots of payouts.

## Collective Investment Schemes and unregulated deposits
Schemes that pool money from the public for returns must be registered with the appropriate regulator. Unregulated deposit-taking is banned under the Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019. Check entities and report suspicious ones on RBI's Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in).

## What to do
Do not invest more, do not recruit others, keep records of all payments and messages, and report to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in, the local police and the Sachet portal.
