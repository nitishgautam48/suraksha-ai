# RBI fraud awareness: banking and digital payment frauds

This summary was prepared for Suraksha AI from the Reserve Bank of India's public-awareness themes (for example its "RBI Kehta Hai" campaign and fraud-awareness booklets). Refer to rbi.org.in for official material.

## Never share confidential credentials
Banks, RBI and payment apps never ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, card PIN, internet-banking password or full card number over phone, SMS, email or chat. Anyone asking for these is a fraudster, even if the caller ID shows a bank name.

## Common modus operandi
- **Fake KYC update**: SMS or call says your account or SIM will be blocked unless you update KYC via a link or app. The link steals credentials or installs a remote-access app.
- **Remote-access apps**: Fraudsters ask you to install screen-sharing apps (such as AnyDesk or TeamViewer) to "help" you, then take over your phone.
- **Fake customer-care numbers**: Numbers listed on search results or social media pretending to be bank or app helplines. Use only numbers from the official website or the back of your card.
- **Lottery, prize and job offers**: You are asked to pay a "processing fee" or "tax" to receive a prize, loan or job.
- **SIM swap**: Fraudsters obtain a duplicate SIM to receive your OTPs. If your mobile network suddenly stops working, contact your operator and bank immediately.
- **Fake loan apps**: Unregistered digital lenders charge hidden fees and harass borrowers. Check that a lender is a bank or an RBI-registered NBFC.

## Unauthorised deposit schemes
Schemes that collect deposits promising high returns without RBI or other regulatory authorisation are illegal under the Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019. You can check and report such entities on RBI's Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in).

## Limited liability if you report quickly
Under RBI's customer-protection framework, if an unauthorised electronic transaction happens because of a third-party breach (not your negligence) and you report it to your bank within three working days, your liability can be zero. Delays increase your liability. Report immediately to your bank and block your card or UPI.

## Complaints
If your bank does not resolve your complaint within 30 days, you can approach the RBI Integrated Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in (contact centre 14448).
