# Social media manipulation tactics in financial scams

## FOMO (fear of missing out)
Messages such as "everyone is earning, don't be left behind" or "last chance" push you to act before thinking.

## Scarcity and urgency
"Only 5 slots left", "offer ends in 30 minutes", "join today". Genuine opportunities do not disappear in minutes. Pause, verify, and discuss with someone you trust.

## Fake success stories
Screenshots of profits, testimonials, luxury cars and travel photos are easy to fake or rent. They are not proof of a legitimate business.

## Emotional manipulation
Appeals to your family's future, guilt, greed, fear or loneliness ("trust me", "do it for your children") are designed to bypass careful judgement.

## Fake authority
Claims like "SEBI approved", "RBI registered", "government scheme" or fake endorsements by celebrities. Verify registrations directly on official regulator websites.

## Pump language
Words like "multibagger", "to the moon", "rocket", "operator buying", "hidden gem" and target prices for little-known stocks or coins are classic pump-and-dump hype.

## How to respond
Do not engage, do not forward, report the account on the platform, and report fraud to 1930 or cybercrime.gov.in. Block and report suspicious numbers through Sanchar Saathi Chakshu.
