# Official guidance quick reference

Short plain-language summaries of public guidance from Indian regulators and government bodies, prepared for Suraksha AI. These are paraphrases, not legal text: the linked official pages are authoritative. The same statements are shown under "What the regulators say" on scan results.

## I4C, Ministry of Home Affairs: National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
There is no such thing as a "digital arrest". Police, CBI, customs or courts never question people or demand money over video calls. Report such calls to 1930.
Official source: https://cybercrime.gov.in/

## SEBI: sebi.gov.in
Fraudsters misuse the names of SEBI and registered brokers. SEBI does not collect money from investors or ask for OTPs; verify any such message on sebi.gov.in.
Official source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/

## SEBI: SEBI investor education
Investments in the securities market carry risk. No SEBI-registered entity is allowed to promise guaranteed or fixed returns.
Official source: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/

## RBI: RBI Alert List of unauthorised forex trading platforms
Forex trading is allowed only through RBI-authorised persons and on authorised platforms or recognised exchanges. RBI publishes an Alert List of unauthorised forex trading platforms and apps.
Official source: https://www.rbi.org.in/

## RBI: RBI Sachet portal
Collecting deposits from the public with promised returns, without regulatory approval, is banned under the Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019. Check and report such schemes on RBI's Sachet portal.
Official source: https://sachet.rbi.org.in/

## SEBI: SEBI list of recognised intermediaries
Only SEBI-registered Investment Advisers and Research Analysts may give paid advice or stock recommendations. Check the registration number on SEBI's list of recognised intermediaries.
Official source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/intermediaries.html

## RBI: RBI Kehta Hai
RBI and banks never ask for your OTP, PIN, CVV or password by call, SMS, email or link. Never share them with anyone.
Official source: https://rbikehtahai.rbi.org.in/

## NPCI: UPI safety guidance
You never need to enter your UPI PIN or scan a QR code to receive money. A PIN is only for sending money.
Official source: https://www.npci.org.in/

## SEBI: SEBI validated UPI handles (@valid) and SEBI Check
SEBI-registered brokers and advisers collect money only through validated UPI IDs containing "@valid". Check a UPI ID with SEBI Check before paying.
Official source: https://www.sebi.gov.in/

## RBI: RBI customer protection in unauthorised electronic banking transactions (2017)
If money leaves your account without your consent, tell your bank at once. Under RBI's customer-protection rules, reporting quickly (within three working days) can limit your liability, in some cases to zero.
Official source: https://www.rbi.org.in/

## Department of Telecommunications: Sanchar Saathi: Chakshu
Report suspected fraud calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages on Chakshu, in the Department of Telecommunications' Sanchar Saathi portal.
Official source: https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/
