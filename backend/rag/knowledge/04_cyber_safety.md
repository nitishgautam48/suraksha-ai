# Cyber safety for investors and families

Summary prepared for Suraksha AI from public awareness resources of the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C, Ministry of Home Affairs), CERT-In and the Department of Telecommunications.

## Reporting cyber fraud
- Call **1930**, the National Cyber Crime Helpline, as soon as possible after a financial fraud.
- File a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**.
- Report suspected fraud calls, SMS and WhatsApp messages on the **Sanchar Saathi** portal using the **Chakshu** facility, and check mobile connections issued in your name.

## "Digital arrest" scams
Fraudsters pose as police, CBI, customs, narcotics or other officials on video calls, claim your parcel contained drugs or your Aadhaar was misused, and keep you on camera for hours while demanding money to "clear your name". There is no such thing as a "digital arrest" under Indian law. Real agencies do not interrogate or arrest people over video calls or ask for money transfers. Disconnect and report to 1930.

## Phishing links and look-alike websites
Phishing messages copy logos and use look-alike domains (for example extra words like "kyc", "verify" or "reward", hyphens, or unusual endings such as .xyz or .top). Shortened links hide the real address. Type official addresses yourself instead of clicking links in messages.

## Deepfakes and impersonation
Scammers use AI-generated videos or voices of celebrities, business leaders or family members to promote fake investment schemes or ask for urgent money. Verify through a separate trusted channel before acting.

## Task and investment-group scams
Victims are added to WhatsApp or Telegram groups, paid small amounts for simple "tasks" (liking videos, rating hotels) to build trust, then asked to "invest" larger sums in fake trading or crypto platforms that show fake profits but never allow withdrawal.

## Basic hygiene
- Use strong, unique passwords and enable two-factor authentication.
- Keep phone and apps updated; install apps only from official stores.
- Do not install APK files sent over chat.
- Never share OTPs or allow screen sharing with unknown persons.
- Talk to a trusted family member before making any urgent payment.
